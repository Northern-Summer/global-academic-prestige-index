# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project space. Participants should use inclusive language, respect differing research and cultural contexts, accept evidence-based correction, and avoid personal attacks.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, doxxing, threats, and publishing private personal data. Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect contributors. Once maintainers are configured, reports can be sent privately through the repository's security contact.
