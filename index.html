Full methodology ↗
01

What is measured

Public education and academic employment statements form employer → training institution edges. PageRank propagates influence over the observed network; formal qualification is a separate decision.

02

Sample boundary

The global view contains a bounded, incomplete source selection. Unresolved identifiers can refer to the same institution. Missing observations and unranked institutions do not imply low influence.

03

Score limits

No complete global denominator is available. Scores are not placement probabilities, teaching quality or causal estimates. Awards and composite scores remain missing without reliable evidence.
