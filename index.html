Full methodology ↗
01

What is measured

How often institutions in the research sample hire tenure-track faculty whose recorded doctorate came from each origin institution, with influence propagated through PageRank.

02

Sample boundary

US faculty hiring relationships from 2011–2020 across academic fields. Doctoral origins can include institutions outside the United States. This is not a census of global academic hiring.

03

Score limits

The source provides aggregated counts, not individual career histories or graduate denominators. Scores are not placement probabilities, teaching quality, or causal estimates.
