    03 / INTERPRETATION

    What this measures

    GAPI summarizes evidence-backed relationships in which a person trained at one institution and later held an academic or research role at another. The edge points from employer to training institution.

    Network influence is not an employment probability, a causal estimate of training quality, or a complete census of global academia. Coverage gaps remain visible and can affect comparisons.

    Awards remain a separate signal. Personal awards, institutional recognition, and technical-history milestones are distinct categories and are not propagated through the mobility network.
