    03 / INTERPRETATION

    What the score means—and what it doesn’t.

    Full methodology ↗
    01

    Observed links

    The model connects a training institution with an employer where a person later held an academic or research role. This demo uses constructed events only.

    02

    Network propagation

    PageRank propagates influence from employers to training institutions. Scores depend on the configured graph and parameters; they are not causal estimates.

    03

    Visible uncertainty

    Missing evidence makes an institution unranked in this fixture. Coverage thresholds help prevent false precision, but do not remove selection bias.
